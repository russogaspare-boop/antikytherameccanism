Nature of the service

antikytheramechanism.app is a free educational and research tool, provided as is. It reproduces the behaviour of the Antikythera mechanism from published data and compares it with simplified modern ephemerides.

Warnings about the results

  • The “true” astronomical positions are computed with reduced-precision algorithms (Meeus): typical errors of a few arc-minutes for Sun and Moon, larger for the planets and for dates far from 2000. They are not an official ephemeris and must not be used for navigation, agriculture, legal purposes or safety-related forecasts.
  • The pointer positions reproduce the ancient machine with its errors: the drift shown is intended and documented.
  • Items marked “hypothesis”, “derived” or “to be verified” are not measured data: see Research and Project status.
  • Dates before 1582 are in the Julian calendar; BC years follow historical numbering (1 BC = astronomical year 0).
