Build It

From the digital model to a real object

The 3D model on this site is kinematic; the printable version is in development. Here is the plan and what already exists.

In development. The starting data exist; the print files are in preparation, as described below.

What already exists

  • 30 measured wheels: tooth count, outer and inner radius, thickness and depth (Freeth et al. 2021, Table S8) — the same ones used by the 3D model.
  • The layout: the axis positions are solved by requiring every pair to mesh (distance = sum of pitch radii), not drawn by hand.
  • The complete kinematic chain: every ratio is checked against the astronomical periods (254/19 for the Moon, 940/4237 for the Saros, etc.).

What is missing to print it

  1. A single module. The ancient teeth are triangular and not uniform; a working print needs redrawn profiles with a standard module while keeping the counts. in development
  2. Tolerances and clearances for FDM/resin printing and for laser-cut sheet metal or acrylic.
  3. Arbors, bushings, plates with real dimensions (the plates at 0, +7.5 and −16 mm are already in the model).
  4. The pin-and-slot group (k1/k2, 1.3 mm eccentricity) and the e3/e4 epicyclic platform: the most delicate part.
  5. Printable dials and scales: zodiac, Egyptian calendar, Metonic and Saros spirals.

Roadmap

  • Milestone 1 — STL/STEP export of the wheels with a standard module and a simplified frame, with assembly instructions. date to be set
  • Milestone 2 — educational kit (reduced version: solar-lunar train and front dial) for schools.
  • Milestone 3 — full model with back dials.

If you are a maker, a mechanic or a technology teacher and want to help with milestone 1, write to us.
