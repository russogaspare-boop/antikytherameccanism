Tomography

The mechanism seen by tomography

Original computed tomography slices of Fragment A, downloaded from the public Harvard Dataverse repository.

Computed tomography (CT) images the object with X-rays from thousands of angles and reconstructs its interior on a computer, layer by layer. This reveals the gears hidden inside the corroded bronze, without touching the fragment.

The images on this page are original slices of scan A6 of Fragment A, the largest. The data were gathered in Athens in 2005 by X-Tek Systems and are published on Harvard Dataverse (Antikythera Fragment A scan A6), together with the paper by Pakzad et al. in PLOS ONE (2018). They were downloaded from there and resized for the web; the text under each image states where it comes from.

The slices are parallel to the gears, one every 0.1 mm: 544 in all, from the front to the back of the fragment. Eight are shown here.
