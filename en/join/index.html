Join the Project

Join the project

Join the development of the first scientifically guided, buildable Antikythera experience.

The calculator is online; the buildable model, the school kit and the conference are in preparation. If you want to be notified when they are ready, or to collaborate, leave your contact.

Who we are looking for

  • Teachers and schools to try the learning paths and tell us what works in class.
  • Museums and institutions interested in an interactive station or a physical version.
  • Researchers to review the data marked “to be verified” and propose corrections.
  • Makers and mechanics for the printable, buildable version.

The button opens your email program with a pre-filled message: no data is sent to or stored by this site. If you prefer, write directly to info@antikytheramechanism.app.

What you get

Only notices about project milestones (new versions, kit, conference). No advertising, no sharing with third parties; you can ask to be removed at any time by replying to a message. Details under Privacy.
