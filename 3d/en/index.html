Three-dimensional kinematic model — measurements from Table S8, Freeth et al. 2021

Antikythera in Three Dimensions

Every gear has the radius, thickness and tooth count measured in the CT scan; the distances between arbors are those imposed by meshing; the motion is the same computing engine as the two-dimensional pages. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan.

What is measured and what is chosen

TO BE VERIFIED — the 2021 paper publishes neither the (x, y) coordinates of the arbors nor the layer-by-layer z depths of the rear train: it states only “five layers in about 8 mm”. Here the rear train occupies about 13 mm because the epicyclic group (e3, e4, e5, k1, k2, e6, e1) is spread over distinct layers for legibility. Should the CT coordinates (Freeth 2006 SI, Wright 2005/2006, IAM 2016) prove available, the plan should be replaced: the code reads it from a single table. Not yet modelled: the front planetary system (Cosmos 2021), the lunar phase device (b0, q1), gear r1 of Fragment D.
